Fit settings

Keys for ferx_fit(settings = list(...)) and the [fit_options] block. The chapter column points at the section that uses the key; the ferx-core fit options page gives the full semantics. A blank Values cell means the key takes a free value (a number, a name or a list) rather than a fixed set; a blank Meaning means the pinned engine documents the key only in terms of other software, which this book does not quote.

vi_sigma_update closed_form As vi_omega_update, for the residual error σ. See How σ is updated. Estimation methods and controlling the fit
